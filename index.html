1. Introduction

Welcome to Sugar Log ("the Application"), developed and operated by ArticOde Innovations ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the sensitive personal and health information of you and your family members.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, stores, and protects your information, and outlines your rights regarding your personal health data.

2. Information We Collect

A. Blood Sugar & Health Data

  • Sugar Readings: Blood sugar level (mg/dL), Test Type/Timing (Fasting, Random, Before Meal, After Meal, Bedtime).
  • Optional Health Notes: Insulin dose (units), and free-text notes about symptoms or meals, if you choose to add them.
  • Measurement Metadata: Measurement date and time.
  • Patient Profiles: Patient name (or nickname), age, and gender.

B. Account & Authentication Data (Optional)

  • Email & Name: If you choose to sign up or sign in using Email or Google Sign-In, we store your email address and display name.
  • User Identifier (UID): A unique identifier generated by Google Firebase Authentication to securely link your data to your account across devices.

C. Advertising & Device Identifiers

  • Advertising ID (GAID): The Application displays ads through Google AdMob. AdMob automatically collects your device's Advertising ID and certain device/network information to serve and measure ads. See Section 6 for full details.

D. Technical & Diagnostic Data

  • Local Preferences: Language preference (English / Urdu / Roman Urdu), notification reminders, theme, and font size settings.
We do NOT collect: Precise GPS location, biometric data (fingerprint/face), IMEI, or MAC address. The only device-level identifier collected is the Advertising ID described above, used solely for ad delivery through Google AdMob.

3. How Your Data Is Stored & Handled

Offline / Local Storage (Default Mode)

If you use the application without logging into an account, 100% of your records and patient profiles remain stored only on your local device, inside the app's private, sandboxed storage (Android SharedPreferences). This storage area is isolated by the Android operating system and is not accessible to other apps.

We cannot access, view, or transfer your offline records — they never leave your device unless you choose to export or share them yourself.

Cloud Synchronization (When Logged In)

If you sign in, your data is synced with Google Cloud Firestore servers. All network communication between the application and Firebase servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS / HTTPS (SSL), and data is encrypted at rest on Google Cloud infrastructure.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to provide and improve the core functionality of the Application:

  • To display blood sugar trend charts, and 7-day / 30-day / 90-day average sugar levels.
  • To calculate and display your logging streak, to help you stay consistent with tracking.
  • To generate clinical PDF reports with doctor's remarks sections for sharing with your physician.
  • To schedule local reminders on your device so you do not miss your sugar checks.
  • To synchronize your records across multiple devices when authenticated.
  • To display advertisements that help keep the Application free (see Section 6).
We NEVER: Sell, rent, lease, or monetize your health data. Use your health information for behavioral advertising. Share your health data with data brokers or pharmaceutical advertisers.

6. Advertising & Google AdMob

Sugar Log is a free application supported in part by advertising, delivered through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process:

  • Your device's Advertising ID (a resettable, non-permanent identifier provided by Android — not your name, email, or health data)
  • General device and app information (device model, app version, general region derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) used to measure ad performance

Advertising data is handled entirely separately from your health records. Your sugar readings, patient profiles, and account information are never shared with AdMob or any advertising network.

You can reset or limit your Advertising ID at any time via your device settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID / Opt out of ad personalization.

7. Permissions Requested

The Application requests only the minimal permissions required to deliver essential features:

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used strictly to deliver local sugar-check reminders and streak reminders configured by you. Reminders are handled locally on-device.
  • Storage / Media (Photo Gallery & Downloads): Used when you choose to export your blood sugar report as a PDF document or save a summary chart into your phone's Gallery. No unrelated files are accessed.

8. Data Retention and Account Deletion

Your Data Rights

Under global privacy standards (including GDPR, CCPA, and Google Play User Data Policies):

  • Right to Access & Export: You can export all your health records at any time into a standard PDF report.
  • Right to Delete (Local): Uninstalling the app or clearing application storage immediately deletes all local offline data.
  • Right to Account Deletion (Cloud): You can request complete deletion of your Firebase account and all associated Firestore patient records directly inside the app, or by emailing articodeinnovations@gmail.com. Upon confirmation, all data is permanently purged within 30 days. See our full Delete Account & Data page for step-by-step instructions.

9. Children's Privacy

The Application is intended for general health tracking by adults and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 without parental consent.

10. Medical Disclaimer

Sugar Log is a self-monitoring log tool, not a diagnostic medical device. The Application does not diagnose diabetes, adjust insulin or medication dosages, or replace professional healthcare consultations. Always seek the advice of your physician or qualified endocrinologist regarding your blood sugar readings.
