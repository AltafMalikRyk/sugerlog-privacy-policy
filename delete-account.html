Overview

You are always in control of your Sugar Log data. You can delete individual readings, delete your entire account and all cloud-synced records, or request deletion by email — whichever you prefer. This page explains all three options.

Option 1 — Delete individual readings (in-app)

You don't need to delete your whole account just to remove a few readings.

1

Open Sugar Log and go to the History screen.

2

Find the reading you want to remove and tap it to open its detail view.

3

Tap Delete and confirm. The reading is removed immediately — from your device, and from the cloud if you are logged in.

Option 2 — Delete your account and all data (in-app)

1

Open Sugar Log and go to Settings → Account → Profile.

2

Tap Delete Account.

3

Confirm when prompted. This permanently deletes your Firebase account, all patient profiles, and every sugar reading stored in the cloud for that account.

Note: If you are using Sugar Log without an account (offline mode), simply uninstalling the app or clearing its storage from your phone's App Settings removes all local data immediately — there is nothing stored in the cloud to delete.
